## The line

> One star. Monday seated me by the drafty door, served cold coffee, then billed me for the whole weekend.

19 words, 104 characters — a one-star review of Monday written as if
Monday were a restaurant: a bad table by the drafty door, a cold dish
(the coffee), and a bill that charges you for the whole weekend. Only
Monday bills you for the weekend it just took away.
